Cairn Launcher

Pure, elegant, and modern Minecraft launcher

A third-party Minecraft launcher written in Rust. There is no Chromium or JVM in the launcher itself; the interface is drawn by the cairn-ui graphics library, with blur and glass composited on the GPU.

  • Rust native
  • cairn-ui
  • GPU glass
  • No JVM shell
  • QuickJS plugins

Principles

What we believe

  1. 01

    Native first

    If a systems language can solve it directly, we don't stack another runtime or shell on top. Smaller binaries, fewer surprises.

  2. 02

    Privacy by default

    No telemetry, no hidden uploads. Your data stays on your device; requests go straight to official services or sources you choose.

  3. 03

    Open by design

    Code, benchmarks and documents live in the open on GitHub. Every performance number can be reproduced, and challenged.
